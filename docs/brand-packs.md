# Brand trouble-code packs

Generic codes (P0xxx, P2xxx, …) are explained by the obdex database. Manufacturer-specific codes (P1xxx, C1xxx, B1xxx, U1xxx, …) mean different things per brand, and sometimes per module, so Obdaryn explains them with **brand packs**.

## Where they live

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `tools/brand_packs/<brand>.py` | The source you edit. Families of codes are written once, in English and Romanian. |
| `src/core/data/dtc/brands/<brand>.json` | Generated by the script above and compiled into the app. Don't edit it by hand. |
| `tools/check_brand_pack.py` | The validator. Run it before every commit. |

```bash
python3 tools/brand_packs/hyundai_kia.py
python3 tools/check_brand_pack.py src/core/data/dtc/brands/hyundai-kia.json
```

The pack id must match the vehicle-profile id in `src/core/data/vehicles/` (for example `hyundai-kia`). The app picks the pack from the car's VIN.

## Rules

A wrong meaning on an airbag or ABS code misleads people about safety systems, so:

1. **Sources are required,** and each must cover that exact code.
   - References are `{"u": url, "o": true}`, where `"o"` flags an official manufacturer document. The app labels those links "official".
   - `"official"`: a Hyundai/Kia document (service bulletin, recall, or chart filed with NHTSA) states the meaning. Needs at least one official link.
   - `"two-sources"`: at least two independent public sources agree. Needs at least two links.
2. **Own words only.** Never paste service-manual text. The pack is GPL; manufacturer text is copyrighted.
3. **Module matters.**
   - Key an entry as `C1604@steering` when the same code means something else in another module.
   - Obdaryn never uses an entry written for a different module than the one that reported the code.
4. **Standard codes are left alone,** unless the brand adds real information, such as which clutch a solenoid letter drives. Such entries set `"override": true`.
5. **Leave it out when in doubt.** If sources disagree or the meaning depends on the year or engine, leave the code out and note why in the research notes below.

## Hyundai/Kia pack (2010–2016: Elantra MD/UD, i30 GD, Ceed JD, Cerato/Forte)

The pack covers 177 entries in English and Romanian, across:
- **ABS/ESC:** wheel-speed sensors, brake switch, yaw sensor, CAN, hydraulic unit;
- **power steering (MDPS);**
- **airbag:** every firing circuit, crash sensors, buckle switches, crash records;
- **TPMS,** body CAN, steering lock and automatic climate control;
- **engine:** knock-sensor bearing detection (P1326), the immobilizer family and the battery sensor;
- **6-speed automatic:** solenoid names.

Meanings were researched in November 2026, mainly from Hyundai/Kia documents filed with NHTSA (static.nhtsa.gov). Every entry lists its sources, and the app shows them as links.

**Left out on purpose**
- **Sources readable only through search-engine summaries:** P1610, P1674, P1675, P169A, C1213, and the TPMS sensor battery codes C1121–C1124.
- **Disputed or uncertain meaning:**
  - P1295, P1693, P1185 and P1186.
  - The B17xx pretensioner codes.
  - The B2510–B2517 lamp codes.
  - C1212, which Kia defines but Hyundai's wording is unconfirmed.
- **Kia-only meanings that would mislead other cars:** P2501–P2503, the battery-sensor meanings.
- **Models or years outside the scope:**
  - P1327, used only on 2019–2021 cars.
  - Parking-assist codes.
  - The 2016+ MDPS codes.

**Help wanted:** codes seen on your own Hyundai/Kia, with the scan-tool output and sources.

## Other packs (researched November 2026)

Every pack follows the same rules and links its sources per code. Counts are distinct codes; some codes have one entry per module.

### Toyota / Lexus (2008–2020: Corolla, Auris, Yaris, RAV4, C-HR, Prius)
- **Coverage:** 96 codes, across ABS/VSC (including the hybrid brake system), airbag, electric power steering, TPMS, hybrid system, engine and immobiliser.
- **Sources:** 26 entries are backed by Toyota/Lexus documents filed with NHTSA. The rest rely on Toyota repair-manual pages hosted by third parties, plus toyota-club.net.
- **Left out:**
  - Side and curtain airbag codes (B1620–B1648, B1820–B1838, B1900–B1908): which side they mean differs between models.
  - The buckle switch codes B1655/B1656.
  - C1253 and P1603: the sources conflict.
  - Single-source codes.
  - The info.myairbags.com list, as a whole: it contradicts Toyota's own documents.
- **Next candidates:** the driver second-stage airbag codes B1810–B1818 are well sourced and can be added.

### Ford (2008–2020: Focus, Fiesta, Kuga/Escape, Mondeo, C-Max)
- **Coverage:** 62 codes, across engine, climate, airbag, SYNC, cluster, ABS, power steering and the PowerShift transmission computer.
- **Sources:**
  - Ford documents filed with NHTSA, including the DPS6 programme and the power-steering recalls.
  - For most codes, two different Ford workshop manuals (Focus 2012 and Fiesta 2014) on unrelated mirror sites. Those are counted as "two sources", not "official".
- **Left out:**
  - Body control (BCM) codes: no second source.
  - Codes found in only one model's manual.
  - Codes that recalls list without stating their meaning (C200B–C200D, C1B00).
  - The DPS6 standard codes: Ford adds nothing to their standard meaning.

### Volkswagen group (2013–2020 MQB: Golf 7, Octavia 3, Leon 3, A3 8V, Passat B8, Tiguan 2)
- **Coverage:** 59 codes, across engine, DSG gearboxes, airbag, ABS, steering, body, gateway, cluster and climate.
- **Sources:** almost all are backed by VW/Audi documents filed with NHTSA.
- **Shared codes:** the data-bus codes U1121–U1123 mean the same in every module, so they are listed once per module.
- **Left out:**
  - Codes found only on Ross-Tech: pelvic belt tensioners, pedestrian sensors, P189A and others.
  - P17BF: its sources word it differently.
  - Codes used only by Bentley, the e-tron, or PQ35-generation cars.
- **Not supported yet:** older TP 2.0 cars.

### Renault / Dacia (2010–2020: Logan, Sandero, Duster, Clio, Megane, Captur)
- **Coverage:** 24 codes, across Bosch ABS/ESP (wheel-speed sensors and tone rings), airbag, power steering, cluster and engine (P1525, P1560).
- **Sources:** two independent sources each. Renault isn't sold in the US, so there are no NHTSA documents to draw on.
- **DF numbers versus what a reader sees:**
  - Renault's own tools show internal "DF" fault numbers, and those change between computer versions.
  - A generic reader sees the code associated with each DF number, in standard form (9040 → B1040). Only those codes are used.
  - Engine computers mostly report standard SAE codes.
- **Left out:**
  - Airbag codes from the older ACU4 tables: their numbering changed between airbag computer generations.
  - Power-steering codes whose tables disagree.
  - Single-tool body, radio and EDC-gearbox codes.


### Suzuki (2005–2014: Swift, SX4, Grand Vitara)

- **Coverage:** 23 codes from the engine computer (power supply, electrical-load sensor, CAN network, immobiliser and keyless start) and the 4-speed automatic gearbox computer.
- **Sources:** the workshop manuals of different Suzuki models (Swift 2005–2010, SX4 2008, Grand Vitara JB416/JB420), each a separately written book. Every code appears in at least two of them. No Suzuki document filed with NHTSA defines a P1 code, so nothing is "official".
- **Not yet confirmed** for the 2010–2017 Swift and later; no workshop manual for those is public. The 2008 SX4 already reports network faults as standard U-codes, and newer cars may too.
- **Left out:** P1615 and P1616 (meaning differs between models), the 4WD low switch codes (one model only), and the 1.3 DDiS diesel codes (one source, and some clash with the petrol meanings). See `tools/brand_packs/suzuki.py`.
- **Modules:** `vehicles/suzuki.json` lists only the standard OBD-II addresses so far (engine, gearbox and 7E4 confirmed from OBDb's recordings), so ABS and airbag codes wait for their addresses.
## Romanian for generic codes

`src/core/data/dtc_generic_ro.json` is a Romanian layer over the obdex codes:
- **Titles:** all 9,533 codes have a Romanian title.
- **Full text:** the 132 most common codes also have a Romanian description, causes and symptoms. Numbers are in Romanian format.

The layer is built by `tools/build_generic_ro.py` from translation files (`{"P0420": "title"}` or full entries). It refuses entries that drop causes or symptoms. To improve a translation, edit the JSON, or rebuild from your own translation files.
